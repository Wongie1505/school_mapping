About This System

The Lilongwe School GIS is an interactive, map-based system for locating and exploring primary and secondary schools across Lilongwe Urban, Malawi. It was built as a GIS research project to make school location data easier to search, filter, and navigate to.

Users can search for schools by name, filter by education level (Primary / Secondary) and ownership (Public / Private), find schools within a chosen distance of any address or their current location, and get driving directions and route options to a selected school.

Map

Browse all schools on an interactive map, search by name or filter by level/ownership, search nearby schools within a radius, and get directions.

Schools

A sortable, searchable table of every school in the system, with a direct link to view each one on the map.

Dashboard

Summary statistics and charts showing the breakdown of schools by level and ownership.
